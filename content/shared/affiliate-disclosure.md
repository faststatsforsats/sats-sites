<p class="lead">Fast Stats for Sats, Fast Facts for Sats, and Fast Acts for Sats are free to read and carry no advertising. They are meant to pay for themselves through affiliate links.</p>

## What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link leads to a product or service and carries a code that identifies this site. If you buy or sign up after following one, the company pays this site a commission. You pay nothing extra.

## How affiliate links are marked

A page that carries affiliate links shows this line above the first one: "Some links here are affiliate links. If you buy through them, this site earns a commission at no cost to you." A page without that line has none.

Product boxes carry the label "affiliate link". Every affiliate link's address begins with /go/ on this site and then forwards to the company.

Links to sources, to a maker's own instructions, and between the three sites are ordinary links. They pay nothing.

## What a commission does not change

A link to a product is not a recommendation to buy it. The guides describe how products work and what to check before buying, and they include products that pay this site nothing. Nothing on these sites is financial advice.
