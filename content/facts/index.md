# How bitcoin works, in plain language

<p class="lead">Each explainer answers one question in its first two sentences, then shows its work. Sats means satoshis, the smallest unit of bitcoin; 100,000,000 sats make one bitcoin.</p>

## Opening October 13, 2026

The first eight explainers cover the basics: what a sat is, how a transaction gets confirmed, what miners do, how a wallet works, the halving schedule, the 21 million cap, how fees are set, and how bitcoin is taxed in the United States. Until launch, this page is a placeholder. Fast Facts carries no price predictions, only how things work.

## The sections

- **The basics.** Sats, transactions, wallets, and keys.
- **The network.** Miners, nodes, the mempool, hashrate, and difficulty.
- **Money and markets.** Halvings, the 21 million cap, inflation, and what the price measures.
- **Rules and taxes.** How bitcoin is taxed and reported in the United States, in general terms. A tax professional should be consulted for your own situation.
- **Safety.** Scams, seed phrases, and what "not your keys" means.
