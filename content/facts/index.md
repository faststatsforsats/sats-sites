## How Bitcoin Works, Without the Jargon

<p class="lead">Bitcoin can sound complicated. Most of it isn't.</p>

Fast Facts for Sats explains the ideas behind Bitcoin in language normal people can understand.

Every guide answers the main question first, then explains why it matters. Live numbers stay current automatically, and the sources are there if you want to dig deeper.

### Start here

- [What is a sat?](/basics/what-is-a-sat/) The smallest piece of a bitcoin, where the name came from, and what one is worth today.
- [How many sats are in a bitcoin?](/basics/how-many-sats-in-a-bitcoin/) Exactly 100,000,000. See the units and learn the simple math.
- [Why count in sats instead of dollars?](/basics/why-count-in-sats/) A different way to look at prices, purchasing power, and what your money buys.
- [What does stacking sats mean?](/basics/stacking-sats/) Why Bitcoiners use the phrase and what they are actually doing.
- [How a bitcoin wallet works](/basics/how-a-wallet-works/) Keys, not coins. What a seed phrase and an address do, and what custodial means.

### The network

- [How Bitcoin fees work](/network/fees-and-the-mempool/) What sat/vB means, why fees rise and fall, and how transactions compete for block space.
- [Who decides Bitcoin's rules?](/network/who-decides-the-rules/) No single person, company, or government. See who proposes changes, who checks the rules, and what a change takes.

### Money and markets

- [Bitcoin halvings explained](/money/halvings/) What gets cut in half, why it happens, and when the next one arrives.
- [The 21 million limit](/money/the-21-million-cap/) Where the number comes from and why nobody can simply create more.
- [Why bitcoin has a price](/money/why-bitcoin-has-a-price/) There is no company behind it and no official price. So where does the number come from?
- [Bitcoin and inflation](/money/bitcoin-and-inflation/) Bitcoin has a fixed supply schedule. The dollar does not. Here is what that means, and what it does not mean.
- [Bitcoin and gold](/money/bitcoin-and-gold/) Two scarce assets with some important similarities and some very big differences.

### Rules and taxes

- [Do I owe taxes on sats?](/rules/taxes-on-sats/) The basic U.S. rules for buying, selling, spending, earning, and holding bitcoin.
