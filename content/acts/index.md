## Buy, earn, store, secure, and use bitcoin

<p class="lead">Bitcoin makes more sense when you use it.</p>

Buy a little. Set up a wallet. Make your first payment. Fast Acts for Sats walks you through each task, with clear steps and the catches explained before they catch you.

Sats are small pieces of bitcoin. There are 100,000,000 sats in one bitcoin, and $25 buys [[live:sats-for:25:usd]] right now ([[live:when]]).

You can start small. You just need to know the next step.

### Buy

[How to buy your first sats](/buy/first-sats/): Choose a service, open an account, and make a small first purchase.

[Bitcoin in an IRA](/buy/bitcoin-ira/): Compare actual bitcoin, bitcoin funds, fees, and who holds the keys.

### Earn

[How to earn sats](/earn/how-to-earn-sats/): Get bitcoin rewards on purchases you already planned to make.

### Store

[How to store sats safely](/store/how-to-store-sats-safely/): Choose a wallet and protect the backup that lets you recover it.

[Best hardware wallets compared](/store/best-hardware-wallets/): Find the features you need without paying for ones you will never use.

[How to set up a hardware wallet](/store/hardware-wallet-setup/): Go from a new device to a checked backup and a successful test payment.

### Secure

[Privacy basics for bitcoiners](/secure/privacy-basics/): Protect your accounts, avoid scams, and share less about what you hold.

### Use

[How to pay with bitcoin](/use/how-to-pay-with-bitcoin/): Scan a code, check the details, and make your first payment.
