# How to buy, earn, store, secure, and use bitcoin

<p class="lead">Five sections, each a set of step-by-step guides. Every guide opens with a link to the explainer behind it on Fast Facts for Sats, so you understand the why before you do the how. Sats means satoshis, the smallest unit of bitcoin; 100,000,000 sats make one bitcoin.</p>

## Opening October 13, 2026

The first eight guides: how to buy your first bitcoin, how to earn sats, how to store bitcoin, the best hardware wallets, how to set up a wallet, bitcoin in an IRA, buying bitcoin privately, and how to pay with bitcoin. Until launch, this page is a placeholder.

## The five sections

- **Buy.** Exchanges, fees, recurring buys, and what the first purchase looks like.
- **Earn.** Ways to earn sats: rewards, work paid in bitcoin, and what to watch for.
- **Store.** Hardware wallets, software wallets, and how custody works.
- **Secure.** Seed phrases, backups, metal plates, and inheritance.
- **Use.** Paying with bitcoin, Lightning, and spending without selling.

Some guides describe products that pay this site a commission when you buy through its links. Each of those pages says so above the first such link, and the products are described, never recommended by amount.
