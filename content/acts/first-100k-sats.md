<p class="lead">100,000 sats is 0.001 bitcoin, worth [[live:money-for:100000]] at the current price ([[live:when]]). A $25 purchase buys [[live:sats-for:25:usd]] before fees.</p>

The chart shows what regular purchases added up to over one past period. It cannot tell you what bitcoin will do next.

These twelve acts help you learn the process and protect what you buy. Do one at a time. Account approval, transfers, and wallet delivery may take longer than the hands-on work.

## Your twelve acts

<ol class="acts">
<li><strong><a href="/buy/first-sats/">Choose a buying service</a>.</strong> Compare fees, availability, and withdrawals. Have your ID and bank details ready. <a href="/buy/first-sats/">How to buy your first sats</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/secure/privacy-basics/">Secure the account</a>.</strong> Use a unique password and stronger sign-in protection. Save recovery codes safely. <a href="/secure/privacy-basics/">Privacy basics for bitcoiners</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/buy/first-sats/">Make a small first buy</a>.</strong> Review the fee and save the confirmation. If available, display the balance in sats. <a href="/buy/first-sats/">How to buy your first sats</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Check what you received.</strong> Compare with the <a href="https://faststatsforsats.com/tools/sats-converter/">sats converter</a> at roughly the same time. Fees, spread, and price movement can explain the difference.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://faststatsforsats.com/charts/twenty-five-a-week/">Consider a recurring buy</a>.</strong> Choose an amount that fits your budget. Review it when your circumstances change. The <a href="https://faststatsforsats.com/charts/twenty-five-a-week/">$25 a week chart</a> shows a historical example.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://fastfactsforsats.com/basics/how-a-wallet-works/">Learn how a wallet works</a>.</strong> Understand keys, receiving addresses, and backups before moving funds. <a href="https://fastfactsforsats.com/basics/how-a-wallet-works/">How a bitcoin wallet works</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/store/how-to-store-sats-safely/">Try a self-custody phone wallet</a>.</strong> Back it up according to its official guide, then transfer a small amount on the correct network. <a href="/store/how-to-store-sats-safely/">How to store sats safely</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/store/best-hardware-wallets/">Consider a hardware wallet</a>.</strong> Compare the cost and features with the amount you want to protect. Buy from the maker or its listed authorized seller. <a href="/store/best-hardware-wallets/">Best hardware wallets compared</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/store/hardware-wallet-setup/">Set it up and verify the backup</a>.</strong> Follow the maker's procedure before funding, then make small test transfers. <a href="/store/hardware-wallet-setup/">How to set up a hardware wallet</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Try sats rewards on a planned purchase.</strong> Compare costs and restrictions first. Extra spending defeats the purpose. <a href="/earn/how-to-earn-sats/">How to earn sats</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/use/how-to-pay-with-bitcoin/">Make one small bitcoin payment</a>.</strong> Try a merchant payment or a Lightning tip and save the receipt. <a href="/use/how-to-pay-with-bitcoin/">How to pay with bitcoin</a>.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://fastfactsforsats.com/rules/taxes-on-sats/">Review your records</a>.</strong> Gather dates, dollars, sats, fees, withdrawals, rewards, and payments. <a href="https://fastfactsforsats.com/rules/taxes-on-sats/">Do I owe taxes on sats?</a></li>
</ol>

## What you will have learned

You will know how to buy, protect an account, choose a wallet, verify a backup, receive rewards, make a payment, and keep records.

Reaching 100,000 sats depends on the amount you choose, fees, rewards, and bitcoin's price. The checklist teaches the process; it does not promise a balance or a profit.

A useful habit is worth more than a hurried start. Take the time to make the steps familiar.
