## What these numbers mean

Hash rate estimates the calculations miners perform each second to compete for the next block. One EH/s is a billion billion calculations per second. The estimate can rise or fall, and individual days can be noisy. Read it alongside the longer history on [Bitcoin hash rate since 2011](/charts/hashrate/).

Block height identifies the latest block in the chain. New blocks arrive about every ten minutes on average, although the wait for any one block varies.

The fee figures are estimates for different confirmation speeds. They are quoted in sats per virtual byte (sat/vB), a measure of transaction size. Your total fee also depends on how large your transaction is. The estimates refresh every ten minutes from [mempool.space](https://mempool.space/); they do not guarantee a confirmation time. [How fees and the mempool work](https://fastfactsforsats.com/network/fees-and-the-mempool/) is explained on [Fast Facts for Sats](https://fastfactsforsats.com/).
