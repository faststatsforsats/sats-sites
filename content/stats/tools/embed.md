<p class="lead">Show a price in sats, or one of the charts, on your own site. Each is a short piece of code that you copy and paste. Both are free and link back here.</p>

## A price in sats

[[embed:badge]]

Enter a price and choose its currency, then copy the code and paste it where the badge should appear. A label, such as the name of a product, is optional.

The badge works out the sats from a bitcoin price that is checked hourly, and it shows the time of that price. There are 100,000,000 sats in one bitcoin. The figure is an estimate at a recent price, not a purchase quote.

If the price cannot be reached, the badge stays a plain link to the [sats converter](/tools/sats-converter/).

## A chart

[[embed:chart:sats-per-dollar]]

This is [Sats per dollar since 2011](/charts/sats-per-dollar/) as another site would show it. Every chart has its own code on its page, under "Embed this chart with a link back". The image refreshes each morning, so the chart on your page stays current.

## What the embeds do

The badge loads from faststatsforsats.com and asks api.faststatsforsats.com for the price. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your reader's browser. A chart is a single image file.

Both are free to use as long as the credit and its link stay in place. The [terms of use](/terms/) cover the details.
