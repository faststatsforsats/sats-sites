## How to read this chart

The line shows the bitcoin equivalent of the median price of a new house sold in the United States. The dollar figure comes from the Census Bureau and HUD through [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/). We divide it by the average bitcoin price for the same quarter.

Median means the middle sale price: half the new houses sold for more and half for less. It is a national measure of new-house sales, so it does not price a particular home or include existing-home sales.

The line can fall because bitcoin rises, new-home prices fall, or both. It can rise when home prices increase or bitcoin falls. The logarithmic scale shows equal multiples at equal distances.

A lower figure means fewer bitcoin were equivalent to that quarter's median sale price. It does not include a buyer's taxes, financing, or closing costs. Use the dates in the table: refreshing the chart daily does not turn quarterly housing data into today's home price.
