## How to read this chart

The chart models a $25 bitcoin purchase every Monday from January 6, 2020, at that day's average price. Each purchase adds sats to the total.

The line shows the sats accumulated. It keeps rising because the model keeps buying and makes no withdrawals. The dollar value of those sats can still fall.

## What the slope tells you

When bitcoin's price is lower, $25 buys more sats and the line climbs faster. When the price is higher, it buys fewer sats and the line climbs more slowly.

A fixed purchase on a regular schedule is often called dollar-cost averaging.

## What the chart cannot tell you

This shows one schedule over one historical period. It does not establish that weekly buying beats buying all at once, predict returns, or remove the risk of loss.

Real purchases can differ because of timing, fees, and the provider's spread.
